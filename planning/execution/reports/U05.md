## Summary of Implementation
Task U05 delivers the complete, server-authoritative frontend screen and interactive controls for Hand Cricket, including role choice upon winning the toss, full innings and target scoreboard, and secret 1..6 delivery locking powered by the reusable `SecretHandoff` component.

1. **Hand Cricket Board (`src/games/hand-cricket/hand-cricket-board.tsx`)**:
   - Toss Phase: Dedicated card surfacing the toss winner with tactile "Bat First" and "Bowl First" buttons.
   - Live Scoreboard: Displays current innings (1st Innings or 2nd Innings / Chase), current ball number (`Ball #N`), active batter and bowler names with role icons, cumulative runs scored, target score, and remaining runs needed to win in chase mode.
   - Secret Delivery Selection: Integrated with `SecretHandoff<number>` for options 1..6 with tactile run buttons.
   - Dual-Mode Handoff & Privacy:
     - Remote Mode: Independent private 1..6 selection with masked waiting indicator ("Delivery Number Locked") and zero leak of opponent's number before reveal.
     - Together Mode: Pass & Play curtain with player confirmation ("I am Player B") between choices, synchronized reveal count-in, and delivery outcome presentation.
   - Delivery Resolution Presentation: Visualizes "WICKET! Batter is OUT on X!" on equal numbers or "+N Runs Scored!" on different numbers, with the next delivery button (`secret.next`).

2. **Match Screen Integration (`src/screens/match.tsx`)**:
   - Wired `cricket.choose-role`, `secret.lock` (with `{ value: 1..6 }`), `secret.reveal`, and `secret.next`.
   - Dynamic mounting when `view?.gameId === "hand-cricket"`.
   - Full support for innings transition (swap roles and set target = firstInningsRuns + 1), immediate chase victory when target reached, defend total victory, equal dismissal draw (`rules_draw`), and rematch actions.

3. **Automated Verification (`tests/e2e/hand-cricket/hand-cricket-screen.test.ts`)**:

   - Verifies live scoreboard and secret handoff options during first innings.
