## 2. Findings and Security Invariants Audit

### A. Snapshot & Event Redaction Verification
- **RPS Secret Privacy**: In `shared/games/rps/engine.ts`, `toPublicView(state, viewer)` strictly omits `state.secretChoices` while `phase === 'locking'`. Clients receive only `lockedSeats: Seat[]`. In together mode, `roundResult` is held until `secret.reveal` is accepted.
- **Hand Cricket Delivery Privacy**: In `shared/games/hand-cricket/engine.ts`, `toPublicView` omits pending delivery numbers (1..6). Only `lockedSeats` is exposed before resolution.
- **Sudoku Opponent Redaction**: In `shared/games/sudoku/engine.ts`, duel mode views for the opponent strictly contain `opponentProgress: { filledCount: number }`. Opponent cell values, notes, correctness, and private solutions are never serialized to the client.
- **Transport Verification**: `worker/matches/match-do.ts` builds broadcast WebSocket messages and HTTP responses exclusively via `engine.toPublicView(state, viewer)`. No raw internal DO SQLite state leaks over the network.

### B. Client-Side Ephemeral Choice Safeguards
- **No Client Persistence**: Raw unrevealed choices are never written to `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, or IndexedDB.
- **Concealment on Focus Loss**: `useSecretConcealment` triggers on `visibilitychange` (`document.hidden`), `window.blur`, and `pagehide`. Any unconfirmed selection is immediately cleared, and a privacy curtain is displayed.
- **Pass & Play Curtain**: In together mode, once Seat 1 locks, the UI immediately conceals the choice and requires an explicit "I am Player 2 (Ready)" barrier before Seat 2's options appear.

### C. Concurrency & Duplicate Lock Prevention
- **Duplicate Lock Rejection**: Submitting a duplicate `secret.lock` from an already locked seat fails with `CHOICE_LOCKED` (`INVALID_ACTION`).
- **Simultaneous Remote Locks**: Independent WebSocket/HTTP commands from both players can lock simultaneously without race conditions due to DO single-threaded transactional execution.

---

## 3. Verdict
The secret handling implementation satisfies all confidentiality, zero-leakage, and handoff privacy contracts. Approved for full game lifecycle integration.
