## Implemented Behavior & Rules
1. **Geometry and Gravity**:
   - 7 columns (0..6) x 6 rows (0..5), where row 0 is the top and row 5 is the bottom floor.
   - Gravity: drops disc to the lowest unoccupied cell in the chosen column (`targetRow` from 5 down to 0).
2. **Move Validation & Error Handling**:
   - Out of turn rejection with `ErrorCode.NOT_YOUR_TURN`.
   - Full column rejection with `ErrorCode.INVALID_ACTION`.
   - Out-of-bounds, fractional, non-integer, or malformed column rejection with `ErrorCode.INVALID_ACTION`.
   - Unknown action type rejection with `ErrorCode.INVALID_ACTION`.
   - Post-completion move rejection with `ErrorCode.MATCH_FINISHED`.
   - Strict immutability: failed validation leaves the input state completely unmutated.
3. **Win Detection & Coordinate Persistence**:
   - Scans 4 directions: Horizontal (`[0, 1]`), Vertical (`[1, 0]`), Diagonal-up (`[-1, 1]`), and Diagonal-down (`[1, 1]`).
   - Identifies contiguous runs >= 4 containing the placed disc.
   - Records the exact 4 winning coordinates in canonical `[row, col][]` format.
   - Emits `disc-dropped` and `game-won` effects.
   - Produces `terminalResult` with `reason: "rules_win"` and scores (`{ A: 1, B: 0 }` or `{ A: 0, B: 1 }`).
4. **Draw Condition & Final Placement Priority**:
   - When all 42 cells are occupied with no 4-in-a-row, game concludes with `winner: null`, `reason: "rules_draw"`, scores `{ A: 0, B: 0 }`, and emits `game-drawn`.
   - Prioritizes win over draw: a 42nd move that completes 4-in-a-row awards a win rather than a draw.
5. **Adapter Implementation**:
   - `connectFourEngineAdapter` exported from `shared/games/connect-four/adapter.ts` implementing `GameEngineAdapter<ConnectFourState, ConnectFourDropPayload, ConnectFourEffect, ConnectFourView>`.
   - All discs in Connect Four are public (`toPublicView` returns the complete board state).
6. **Provenance & License Notice**:
   - Logic inspired by `@devshareacademy/connect-four` (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 Dev Share Academy). Implemented as a clean pure reducer without mutable singletons.
