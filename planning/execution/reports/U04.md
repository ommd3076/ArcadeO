## Summary of Implementation
Task U04 implements tactile, fully responsive, server-authoritative frontend boards and interactive controls for Dots & Boxes and SOS, mounted in the `MatchScreen` workflow.

1. **Dots & Boxes Board (`src/games/dots-boxes/dots-boxes-board.tsx`)**:
   - 5x5 dot grid with 4x4 claimable boxes.
   - Dual input modes:
     - Direct edge selection with generous touch-friendly hit areas (28px height/width hit area with 3px/6px centered pill lines).
     - Alternative dot selection: tapping an initial dot highlights it with an illuminated glow; tapping an adjacent orthogonal dot completes the edge placement.
   - Live score strip showing Player A and Player B claimed boxes with player accent badges.
   - Claimed box display with smooth scale-in animation and claiming player's letter mark.

2. **SOS Board (`src/games/sos/sos-board.tsx`)**:
   - 5x5 cell grid (25 cells) with tactile touch buttons (min 48px square cells).
   - Letter selector toggle: tactile pill buttons to choose "S" or "O" before tapping an empty cell.
   - SVG strike-through lines: automatically draws high-visibility connecting lines across the 3 cells of any completed SOS sequence, colored according to the claiming player's accent (mint/teal for A, violet/pink for B).
   - Live score strip and turn retention indicator.

3. **Match Screen Integration (`src/screens/match.tsx`)**:
   - Wired `dots-boxes.edge` and `sos.place` action handlers.
   - Dynamic board mounting based on `view?.gameId === "dots-boxes"` and `view?.gameId === "sos"`.
   - Full lifecycle compatibility: readiness check, mutual turns, score updates, turn retention upon claiming boxes/forming SOS lines, resignation sheet, and terminal result card.

4. **Automated Verification (`tests/e2e/dots-boxes/` & `tests/e2e/sos/`)**:
   - `tests/e2e/dots-boxes/dots-boxes-screen.test.ts`: Verifies 5x5 dots, edge hit areas, scores, claimed boxes, and dynamic MatchScreen mounting.
   - `tests/e2e/sos/sos-screen.test.ts`: Verifies 5x5 cells, S/O selector toggle, score counter, placed letters, SVG strike-through lines, and dynamic MatchScreen mounting.
