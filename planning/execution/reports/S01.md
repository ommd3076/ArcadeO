## Behavior and Implementation Details
1. **Source Bank Qualification**:
   - Verified SHA-256 checksums and exact byte counts against planning specifications in `planning/ASSET-INVENTORY.md`:
     - `easy.txt`: 10,000,000 bytes, `789aab6f52cc4588e0c3aa4269ad3282cfda7e1cc79ea693d16507decde3fc50`
     - `medium.txt`: 35,264,300 bytes, `c2a5f8fac99dcf215b25d46ece47f346375e51730b182de70a6f8f2107b2b38d`
     - `hard.txt`: 32,159,200 bytes, `abcff1512411e601abd5e861c60add5be5d877739b6faa249ee86960f5ad85e9`
     - `diabolical.txt`: 11,968,100 bytes, `08553d0c1145ea4d7c13008040f47ea8205d21fe1eaf8f4ab17a1a6981928b35` (mapped to Expert bucket).
2. **Deterministic Sampling and Numbering**:
   - Selected exactly 250 puzzles per bucket (1,000 total) across each source bank.
   - Puzzles are assigned stable IDs `easy-001`..`easy-250`, `medium-001`..`medium-250`, `hard-001`..`hard-250`, and `expert-001`..`expert-250`.
   - Numbers are strictly 1..250 within each bucket.
   - Verified that all 1,000 puzzle givens and hashes are globally unique (0 duplicate clues or collisions).
3. **Independent Solution Count and Derivation**:
   - An exact bitmask backtracking solver with Minimum Remaining Values (MRV) heuristic was executed on every puzzle, searching up to 2 solutions.
   - All 1,000 selected puzzles were verified to have **EXACTLY ONE** unique solution.
   - Private 81-character solutions were captured and stored in `content/sudoku/solutions.json`.
4. **Public Privacy Protection**:
   - `content/sudoku/catalog.json` contains only `{ puzzleId, bucket, number, rating, givens, sourceId }`.
   - Zero solution fields or answer data exist in the catalog file, ensuring no solution leaks to public client bundles.
