## Implemented Behavior & Rules

### 1. Dots & Boxes Pure Engine (`shared/games/dots-boxes/`)
- **Geometry & Canonicalization**:
  - 5x5 dot grid (rows 0..4, cols 0..4) yielding 4x4 boxes (16 total boxes, 40 orthogonal edges).
  - Orthogonal edges connect points with Manhattan distance 1 (`|r1 - r2| + |c1 - c2| === 1`).
  - Endpoints canonicalized such that `(r1, c1) < (r2, c2)`, preventing duplicate edges regardless of input coordinate order.
- **Box Closure & Scoring**:
  - Placing an edge evaluates the 1 or 2 adjacent boxes.
  - A box is completed when all 4 of its bounding edges exist.
  - Each newly completed box awards 1 point and ownership to the placer (`boxes[row][col] = actorSeat`).
  - Simultaneous two-box closure: an internal edge completing two adjacent boxes awards 2 points and both box ownerships to the placer.
- **Turn Retention**:
  - If 1 or 2 boxes are closed, the placer retains the turn (turn does not change).
  - If 0 boxes are closed, turn alternates to the opponent (`turn-changed` emitted).
- **Game Conclusion**:
  - Match concludes when all 40 edges are placed.
  - If `scores.A > scores.B`, winner is "A" (`rules_win`, emits `game-won`).
  - If `scores.B > scores.A`, winner is "B" (`rules_win`, emits `game-won`).
  - If `scores.A === scores.B`, winner is `null` (`rules_draw`, emits `game-drawn`).
- **Adapter**:
  - Exported as `dotsBoxesEngineAdapter` adhering to `GameEngineAdapter<DotsBoxesState, DotsBoxesEdgePayload, DotsBoxesEffect, DotsBoxesView>`.

### 2. SOS Pure Engine (`shared/games/sos/`)
- **Grid & Placement**:
  - 5x5 board (25 cells total).
  - Players place letter `"S"` or `"O"` in any unoccupied cell.
- **Contiguous 3-Cell S-O-S Line Detection**:
  - Evaluates all contiguous 3-cell S-O-S lines containing the newly placed letter in all 4 axes: Horizontal, Vertical, Diagonal-Down, and Diagonal-Up.
  - Handles placement of `"O"` as line center, and placement of `"S"` as line endpoint.
  - Canonicalizes line endpoints `(r1, c1) < (r2, c2)` to prevent duplicate or reversed counting.
  - Allows letter sharing and line overlap (e.g. S-O-S-O-S sequence or cross intersections).
- **Scoring & Turn Retention**:
  - Each newly formed line awards 1 point and line ownership to the placer.
  - Multiple lines on a single placement (e.g. orthogonal cross) award 1 point per line.
  - If at least 1 line is formed, the placer retains the turn. If 0 lines formed, turn alternates.
- **Game Conclusion**:
  - Match concludes when all 25 cells are occupied.
  - Higher score wins (`rules_win`, emits `game-won`); equal scores draw (`rules_draw`, emits `game-drawn`).
- **Adapter**:
  - Exported as `sosEngineAdapter` adhering to `GameEngineAdapter<SOSState, SOSPlacePayload, SOSEffect, SOSView>`.

### 3. Protocol & Error Handling
- Rejection of moves when match is finished (`ErrorCode.MATCH_FINISHED`).
- Rejection of out-of-turn moves (`ErrorCode.NOT_YOUR_TURN`).
- Rejection of invalid action types, out-of-bounds coordinates, non-integers, invalid letters, or non-adjacent points (`ErrorCode.INVALID_ACTION`).
- Rejection of already placed edges or occupied cells (`ErrorCode.INVALID_ACTION`).
- Strict state immutability: failed validation leaves state completely unmutated.
