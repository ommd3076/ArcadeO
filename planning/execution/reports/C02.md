## 2. Deliverables Implemented

### Reusable Secret Handoff (`src/components/secret-round/secret-handoff.tsx`)
- General component designed for simultaneous/secret choice games (Rock Paper Scissors, Hand Cricket).
- **Together Mode (Pass & Play)**:
  1. `ready-seat-1`: Active readiness barrier ensuring Seat 2 looks away.
  2. `choosing-seat-1`: Tactile choice grid (min 44×44px touch targets).
  3. `curtain-pass-to-2`: Immediate concealment of Seat 1's choice with a "Pass phone to Player 2" screen.
  4. `ready-seat-2`: Readiness barrier for Seat 2.
  5. `choosing-seat-2`: Seat 2 makes secret choice (Seat 1's choice remains masked).
  6. `both-locked-reveal`: Screen shows "Both choices locked!" with prominent "Reveal Outcome" button (`secret.reveal`).
  7. `revealed-outcome`: 3-beat synchronized count-in animation before displaying round outcome, followed by "Next Round" (`secret.next`).
- **Remote Mode**:
  1. Local player selects and locks choice (`secret.lock`).
  2. Locked-waiting state: "Choice Locked" indicator with animated waiting spinner ("Waiting for [Opponent]...").
  3. Receives server event once both lock -> triggers reveal and outcome display.
- **Privacy & Concealment**:
  - `useSecretConcealment`: Listens to `visibilitychange` (`document.hidden`), `window.blur`, and `pagehide`. Automatically conceals any unconfirmed selection preview with a "Screen Concealed for Privacy" overlay and "Resume & Unmask" barrier.
  - Zero storage of raw unrevealed choices in local storage or unmasked state.
  - Turn alternation for first chooser (`firstChooserSeat`).
