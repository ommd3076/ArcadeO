## Behavior and Implementation Details

### 1. Ludo Engine
- **Token Model**: 4 tokens per seat (`0..3`) for seats `'A'` and `'B'`.
- **Progress Coordinates**:
  - `-1`: Yard.
  - `0..50`: Shared track ring indices (`ringIndex = (startSquare + progress) % 52`).
  - `51..56`: Private home lane (`56` = finished / home).
  - Exact finish at 56 required; moves overshooting 56 are rejected.
- **Fixed Geometry**:
  - Start squares: Seat A at ring 0 (`[6,1]`), Seat B at ring 26 (`[8,13]`).
  - 8 safe squares: `0, 8, 13, 21, 26, 34, 39, 47`.
  - 52 ring coordinates mapped to zero-based 15x15 grid coordinates.
  - Private home lanes: A at `[7,1]..[7,6]`, B at `[7,13]..[7,8]`. Home lanes cannot capture or be captured.
- **Rules & Mechanics**:
  - Yard entry: Roll of 6 enters a yard token onto start square (progress 0); does not advance further cells on that move.
  - Unsafe landing: Captures the **lowest-ID opposing token only** (`Math.min(...matchingOppIds)`), returning it to yard (`-1`). Remaining opposing tokens stay (mixed occupancy permitted).
  - Safe squares: Protect tokens from capture; mixed occupancy permitted.
  - Third consecutive six: Ignored (causes no token movement) and grants another roll until a 1–5 appears. Streak is capped at 2. A non-six or turn transfer resets streak to 0.
  - Bonus rolls: Granted on rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or reaching home (`56`). Bonuses do not stack (maximum 1 bonus roll per turn). A non-six capture/home bonus continues the turn with a reset streak of 0.
  - Win condition: First to reach home with all 4 tokens wins immediately, terminating the match before any bonus roll.
  - Selection: If no legal moves, turn passes (or rerolls if 6). If exactly 1 legal move, it is auto-selected immediately within `dice.roll`. If multiple legal moves, phase transitions to `choose-token` awaiting `ludo.move`.
  - Effects emitted: `dice-rolled`, `token-moved`, `token-captured`, `token-entered-home`, `turn-changed`, `game-won`.

### 2. Snakes & Ladders Engine
- **Board Geometry**: 10x10 board, serpentine numbering 1 to 100 with positions 0 (off-board) to 100 (win).
- **Exact 100 Arrival**: Exact arrival at 100 required to win. Overshoot stays in place and passes turn to opponent.
- **No Bonus on Six**: Rolling a 6 moves normally and transfers turn immediately to opponent.
- **Transitions**:
  - Ladders: `2->23`, `8->34`, `20->41`, `32->51`, `49->70`, `60->83`, `73->94`.
  - Snakes: `27->5`, `39->16`, `56->35`, `68->46`, `79->58`, `88->66`, `97->76`.
  - Landing on transition origin triggers slide to destination once.
- **Shared Occupancy**: Both tokens can share squares without capture.
- **Effects emitted**: `dice-rolled`, `token-advanced`, `snake-bitten`, `ladder-climbed`, `turn-changed`, `game-won`.
