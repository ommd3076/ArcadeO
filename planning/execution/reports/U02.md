## 2. Deliverables Implemented

### Rock Paper Scissors Board (`src/games/rps/rps-board.tsx`)
- Displays target wins banner ("First to {targetWins} wins") and running score comparison between Player A and Player B.
- Embeds `SecretHandoff<RPSChoice>` component with tactile cards for Rock (✊), Paper (✋), and Scissors (✌️).
- In remote mode: local choice locking (`secret.lock`) displays "Choice Locked" and animated waiting spinner until the opponent locks.
- In together mode: implements the full Pass & Play cycle (Seat 1 chooses -> curtain hides choice and prompts phone pass -> Seat 2 chooses -> both locked reveal -> 3-beat synchronized count-in animation -> outcome shown -> Next round progression).
- Outcome display: clear announcement of round winner (or draw when both choose the same hand) with highlighted choice symbols.
- Advances round smoothly upon mutual `secret.next` action.

### Match Screen Dynamic Mount (`src/screens/match.tsx`)
- Detects `view.gameId === "rock-paper-scissors"` and dynamically renders `RPSBoard` with match session integration.
- Dispatches `secret.lock`, `secret.reveal`, and `secret.next` through `useMatchSession`.
- Dynamic gameId in rematch creation preserving the current game and mode.
