## Implemented Behavior & Rules
1. **Matchup Evaluation & Rules**:
   - Pure evaluator `evaluateRPS(choiceA, choiceB)`: Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock. Identical choices resolve to `"draw"`.
   - Supports 3 match formats: `"best-of-3"` (target 2 wins, default), `"best-of-5"` (target 3 wins), and `"best-of-7"` (target 4 wins).
   - Draws award no score points to either seat and complete the secret round normally with no total-round cap.
2. **Move Validation & Error Handling**:
   - `secret.lock` accepted during `"locking"` phase only.
   - Prevents duplicate lock from same seat with `ErrorCode.CHOICE_LOCKED`.
   - Validates choice payload (`"rock" | "paper" | "scissors"`) rejecting invalid choices with `ErrorCode.INVALID_ACTION`.
   - Rejects actions attempted after game completion with `ErrorCode.MATCH_FINISHED`.
   - Strict immutability: failed validation leaves input state completely unmutated.
3. **Phases & Round Progression**:
   - `"locking"`: Players submit their secret choices independently. When both seats have locked, outcomes are evaluated.
   - `"resolved"`: Round outcome is computed (`scores`, `roundResult`).
     - In remote mode, `roundResult` is immediately exposed to both players.
     - In together mode, `roundResult` is withheld until `secret.reveal` is performed (`revealed = true`).
     - Both players signal `secret.next` (in together mode, each or both can confirm) to increment `roundId`, clear secret choices, reset lockedSeats and readiness, and transition back to `"locking"`.
   - `"terminal"`: When a player reaches `targetWins`, match transitions to `"terminal"`, emits `game-won`, and stores `terminalResult` (`winner`, `reason: "rules_win"`, final scores, `finishedAt`).
4. **Privacy & Redaction Matrix Guarantees**:
   - Crucial privacy guarantee: `secretChoices` are kept server-internal and never exposed in `toPublicView`.
   - In `"locking"` phase, `toPublicView` exposes only `lockedSeats` (e.g. `['A']`) so clients know who has locked without disclosing the choice.
   - In together mode, `roundResult` is withheld as `null` until `revealed === true`.
5. **Adapter Implementation**:
   - `rpsEngineAdapter` exported from `shared/games/rps/adapter.ts` implementing `GameEngineAdapter<RPSState, SecretLockPayload, RPSEffect, RPSView>`.
