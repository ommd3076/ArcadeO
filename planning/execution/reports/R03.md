## 1. Rules Consistency Audit (`consistency-reviewer`)
Audited all 8 game engines and adapters for deterministic state transitions, legal actions, and terminal invariants:
1. **Connect Four (`shared/games/connect-four/`)**:
   - Gravity: Dropping into columns 0..6 correctly places discs at the lowest unoccupied row. Full columns reject moves with `INVALID_ACTION`.
   - Win vs Draw precedence: 42nd disc creating a 4-in-a-row correctly declares a win before a draw.
2. **Rock Paper Scissors (`shared/games/rps/`)**:
   - All 9 choice combinations evaluate deterministically. Ties award no points.
   - Formats best-of-3 (target 2), best-of-5 (target 3), and best-of-7 (target 4) terminate exactly at the win threshold.
3. **Ludo (`shared/games/ludo/`)**:
   - Safe squares (0, 8, 13, 21, 26, 34, 39, 47) prohibit capture. Unsafe captures target lowest token ID only.
   - Third consecutive six is ignored without advancing tokens.
4. **Snakes & Ladders (`shared/games/snakes-and-ladders/`)**:
   - Exact roll to 100 required; overshoots hold in place.
   - Origin-only slides for 7 fixed snakes and 7 fixed ladders. Rolling 6 awards no bonus roll.
5. **Dots & Boxes (`shared/games/dots-boxes/`)**:
   - Canonical edge ordering prevents duplicate reversed lines.
   - Completing 1 or 2 boxes awards 1 point per box and grants turn retention once.
6. **SOS (`shared/games/sos/`)**:
   - Contiguous 3-letter S-O-S detection across all 4 axes with shared letter overlap support.
   - Placer retains turn on completing at least 1 line.
7. **Hand Cricket (`shared/games/hand-cricket/`)**:
   - Toss role choice (Bat vs Bowl) correctly initializes 1st innings.
   - Identical choices produce immediate OUT. Defending, chasing, and equal-dismissal draws verified.
8. **Sudoku (`shared/games/sudoku/`)**:
   - Givens are strictly immutable. Bitmask pencil notes and undo stack function reversibly.
   - 1,000 launch puzzles independently solved and verified for unique solutions.

## 2. Final Security Audit (`R03-S` / `security-reviewer`)
1. **Secret Redaction**:
   - Rock Paper Scissors: `toPublicView` excludes `secretChoices` during the locking phase. Viewers only see `lockedSeats`.
   - Hand Cricket: Secret delivery numbers 1..6 are masked until both seats lock and the delivery resolves.
   - Sudoku: Practice mode solution checking keeps the full solution private on the server; duels expose only opponent filled cell counts (`filledCount`), never cell entries or notes.
2. **Authentication & Session Security**:
   - PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and 600,000 iterations + 16-byte cryptographically secure salt.
   - 256-bit session tokens stored as SHA-256 hashes in D1; session cookies use `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Path=/`.
   - Cryptographically derived CSRF tokens verified on all mutating routes.
   - Rate limiting enforced at 5 failed attempts per account per 15 minutes.
3. **Optimistic Concurrency & Invariants**:
   - Preference updates enforce `preferenceVersion` optimistic concurrency.
   - Atomic constraint: Players A and B cannot select the same accent color family (`distinct-accents`).
   - Service worker precaches public shell assets only; strictly bypasses all API routes, WebSockets, and state projections.

All eight game rules and security invariants are complete, consistent, and strictly verified against specification contracts.
